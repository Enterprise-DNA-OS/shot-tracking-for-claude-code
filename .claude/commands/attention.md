# attention

Run `node scripts/shots.mjs attention --json`. Group by category: over bid, waiting for review, waiting on the client, client notes not addressed, change orders undecided, due soon with nothing approved, approved but not delivered, nobody assigned, artists overbooked, rebate. Lead with the item that costs money or a deadline first. One line each, with the record reference.
